Aurevia Maison – Chapter 1

Billionaire Romance / Interactive

Choice-driven sample · Free + Premium options

Elena has been standing outside Granny Winona’s door for three minutes when it finally opens.

“Granny Winona!”

Winona smiles. “Morning, child.”

Elena brings in the energy drinks she bought for her. Just as she finishes putting them in the fridge, Hailey bursts out of the bedroom, camera in hand, hair half-tied.

“Adrian Vale’s flight lands in twenty minutes. I need to get to the airport!”

Choice 1
  1. “I’m coming with you!”
  2. “Wait, let me grab my better camera first.”
  3. [Premium] “I’ll drive us — it’ll be faster.”

At the airport, the crowd is massive. Elena is trying to find a good angle when she crashes into a man’s chest. Her camera falls. A hand catches it before it hits the ground.

It’s Damian Cross.

Choice 2
  1. “Thank you! Sorry!” (take the camera and run)
  2. “Wow… you have good reflexes.”
  3. [Premium] Hold his gaze a moment longer and say, “Have we met before?”

Adrian appears at the top of the escalator with Mia. The crowd explodes. Elena is taking pictures when the pushing starts. She loses her balance and falls — right at Adrian’s feet.

Security pulls her up. Adrian looks down at her for a long second. Something flickers in his eyes. Then he turns and walks away.

Choice 3
  1. Stay frozen and just stare after him.
  2. Whisper “I’m so sorry” loud enough for him to hear.
  3. [Premium] Meet his eyes and say clearly, “I didn’t mean to fall.”

Later that night, after work, Elena stops outside the gates of Aurevia Maison. A familiar voice comes from behind her.

“I see. It’s really you.”

Adrian Vale. Alone.

He calls her a stalker. Says she’s obsessed. Then delivers the line:

“I don’t deal with people like you. Dry bones. Get lost.”

Choice 4 – Major Branch Point
  1. Break down crying and walk away without a word.
  2. Force yourself to look him in the eye and say, “I’m not a stalker. I just… admire you.”
  3. [Premium] Straighten your back and reply coldly, “You don’t know anything about me.”

This choice changes how Adrian sees Elena and influences their future interactions.
